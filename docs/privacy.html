Party Lights: DJ & Strobe – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Party Lights does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

Microphone

If you choose "Music (mic)" as the beat source, the app listens to sound only to detect the beat and make the lights pulse. Audio is processed live on your phone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only while the light show is running.

Flashlight

If you turn on the flashlight strobe, the app switches the camera LED on and off. It does not take photos or video.

Party sync (local network)

If you use Party sync, the app sends light settings (effect, colours, speed and timing) to other phones on the same Wi‑Fi network. This information stays on your local network and contains no personal data.
